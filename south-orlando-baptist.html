Sermon recap · Romans 16:1–27

Living the Gospel in Community

On Sunday, Pastor David Crowe brought us to the final chapter of Romans, finishing a book our church has labored through together for years. Paul closes his letter with a long list of names, and those names paint a picture of what it looks like to live out the gospel together.

1. Appreciate faithful servants (vv. 1–16). Phoebe, Prisca and Aquila, and many others served Paul and the churches faithfully. Pastor Dave shared how people like Juanita Gaines, a pastor's wife in his first church, built him up in ways no seminary degree could.

2. Avoid divisive people (vv. 17–20). Paul warns against those who cause divisions with smooth talk and flattery. When a church stands together in unity, God crushes Satan under its feet.

3. Advance the gospel mission (vv. 21–27). The gospel is still going out to all nations. Together, through giving, missions and partnership, we can do far more than any of us could alone.

God didn't save us to be lone ranger Christians. Missed Sunday? Watch the full message on our YouTube channel.
