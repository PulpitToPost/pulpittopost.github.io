Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Pulpit to Post (operated by Joel Rodriguez, 3564 Avalon Park E Blvd, Ste 1-A1032, Orlando, FL 32828) collects, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • What you tell us on our form: your name, role, email, phone (optional), church name, website, YouTube channel, preacher names, service times, address, colors, logo link, posting preferences and notes.
  • Your sermon videos: public videos from the YouTube channel you give us, the transcripts we make from them, and the graphics and clips we create.
  • Payment details: handled by Stripe. We see your name, email, plan and payment status, but never your full card or bank number.
  • Connected accounts: when you connect Facebook or Instagram, Meta gives us an access token and the IDs and names of the Page and Instagram account you selected. We use these only to publish your posts and to confirm they went out.
  • Emails you send us and basic records of what we've sent you.

How we use it

Only to run the Service: to make your free sample, create and publish your weekly posts, send you samples, approvals, receipts and service emails, provide support, and keep records required for billing and taxes. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Service providers we use

We share only what each one needs to do its job: GitHub (runs our automation and hosts our website and sample pages), Google (Gmail, for email), Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI that helps write captions and pick quotes from transcripts), Web3Forms (delivers our website form), Meta (publishing to Facebook and Instagram), and a proxy provider used when downloading your videos from YouTube.

Sample pages

Sample and preview pages are published at hard-to-guess web addresses that are hidden from search engines and shared only with you. Anyone you forward the link to can view it. Ask us and we'll delete your sample page.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're a subscriber. If you never subscribe, or after you cancel, we delete your transcripts, graphics, clips and connected-account tokens within 90 days, except billing records we must keep for tax purposes.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information. Email joelspulpittopost@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" from the email address you used with us. We'll confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. You can also remove Pulpit to Post's access to your accounts yourself: in Facebook, go to Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) and remove Pulpit to Post. Doing this stops all posting right away.

Security

Account tokens and keys are stored as encrypted secrets, and we never ask for your passwords. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to what's needed to run the Service.

Children

The Service is for churches and their staff, not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We'll post any changes here and update the date above. Questions: joelspulpittopost@gmail.com, or by mail to the address above.
